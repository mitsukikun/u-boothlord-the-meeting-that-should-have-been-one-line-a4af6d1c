One line that replaces the meeting outright: "The budget is approved, the deadline is Friday, and this sentence just replaced the meeting you're sitting in." It delivers the meeting's entire payload — the decision and the deadline — and then, in the same breath, cancels the meeting itself, so there is nothing left to meet about.
